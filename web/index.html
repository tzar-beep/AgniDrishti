Limitations

  • FIRMS records thermal anomalies, not cause; prescribed and agricultural burns are included.
  • Weather is ERA5-based (~0.25°), sampled at cell centroids; terrain-driven microclimate is not resolved.
  • Cloud, smoke and canopy obscure detections, so some negatives are missed fires.
  • Class weighting inflates probabilities; treat P as a relative ranking.
  • Hindcast for 2022–2024 only; no live forecast inputs.
