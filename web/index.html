About this model

Probability that a VIIRS fire hotspot (nominal/high confidence) is detected in each 0.25° cell during the 7 days from the selected Monday. Model: XGBoost on weather, terrain and fire-history features; trained 2015–2021, validated 2022–2023, tested 2024.

Limitations

  • FIRMS detects heat, not cause: controlled burns and crop-residue fires count too.
  • Weather is ERA5-based at ~0.25°; local valley/ridge conditions are not resolved.
  • Cloud and canopy cover can hide fires, so labels miss some events.
  • Probabilities from a class-weighted model are not perfectly calibrated.
